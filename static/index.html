Organ Relay

The cooler that coordinates itself. An invisible interface for donor-organ transport: sensing replaces data entry, light and voice replace screens, and humans are only interrupted when a decision is needed.

📦 BoxPhone inside the cooler. Glows green, amber or red; speaks on change; reports shocks and GPS.
🚗 CourierStays in a pocket. Buzz patterns and spoken instructions only.
📞 Coordinator phoneSilent until a shipment is at risk, then it rings. Answer by voice.
🏥 OR ambientRoom light warms as the organ approaches; spoken 60/30/10-minute calls.
🗺️ Judges' mapWhat the system sees: route, margin, auto-logged custody chain.
🎛️ Control panelBackstage "Wizard of Oz" controls: clock, delays, temperature, shocks.

Demo script (about 3 minutes)

  1. Open Box, Coordinator and OR on separate devices, tap start on each. Put the map on the projector.
  2. On Control, press Play. The box glows green; handoffs log themselves as it moves. Point out that nobody has typed anything.
  3. Press Flight cancelled (+5h). The box turns red and speaks; the clock slows; the coordinator phone rings.
  4. Answer and say "reroute" (or "charter"). The box goes green, the courier phone buzzes, the old OR is told to stand down and the new one is told the organ is inbound.
  5. Speed up to 900× and let it deliver. Show the custody chain on the map.
